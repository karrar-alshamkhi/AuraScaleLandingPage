Activation

Activate your license.

After purchasing through Paddle, follow these steps to unlock Aura Scale on your PC.

How to activate

Three simple steps.

1

Complete your purchase

Buy a license from the Pricing page using Paddle. You will receive a confirmation email with your license key shortly after payment.

2

Open Aura Scale

Launch the app on your PC. If your trial has expired, the activation screen will appear automatically. You can also access it from the app menu.

3

Enter your license key

Paste the license key from your email into the activation dialog and click Activate. The app will unlock immediately.

Common questions

Activation help.

What happens when my trial expires?

When the 10-day trial ends, Aura Scale will show an activation prompt. You will not be able to process images until a valid license key is entered.

Where do I find my license key?

Your license key is included in the purchase confirmation email sent by Paddle. Check your inbox and spam folder. If you cannot find it, contact us with your order ID.

Can I move my license to a new PC?

A license is tied to one machine. If you replace your PC, contact support and we can help transfer your activation.

My activation is not working — what should I do?

Make sure you are entering the key exactly as it appears in your email, with no extra spaces. If the issue continues, reach out with your order details.
