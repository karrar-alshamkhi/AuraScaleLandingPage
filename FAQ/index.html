FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about the trial, purchase, and activation.

Trial

About the free trial.

How long is the free trial?

The free trial lasts 10 days from the moment you first launch Aura Scale on your PC.

Do I need to provide payment details to start the trial?

No. Just download and install the app. No credit card or account is required to start the 10-day trial.

What features are available during the trial?

You have access to all features during the trial period — AI upscaling, noise reduction, and detail recovery.

What happens when the trial expires?

When the 10-day trial ends, the app will require a valid license key to continue. You can purchase a license from the Pricing page.

Purchase

Buying a license.

How do I buy Aura Scale?

Click the Buy with Paddle button on the Pricing page. You will be taken to a secure Paddle checkout page where you can pay by card or PayPal.

Is this a one-time purchase or a subscription?

It is a one-time purchase. You pay once and get a lifetime license for the current major version of Aura Scale.

What do I receive after purchase?

Paddle will send you a confirmation email containing your order details and your unique license key.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Paddle, a trusted payment platform used by thousands of software companies worldwide.

Activation

Activating your license.

How do I activate Aura Scale after purchasing?

Open the app, click Activate, and enter the license key from your purchase email. See the full guide on the Activation page.

Does Aura Scale require an internet connection to activate?

Activation requires a one-time internet connection to validate your license key. After that, the app works fully offline.

Can I use my license on more than one computer?

Each license is valid for one machine. Contact us if you need to transfer your license to a new PC.

I lost my license key — what should I do?

Check your original purchase confirmation email. If you cannot find it, contact support with your Paddle order ID and we will resend your key.

Still have questions?

We are here to help.

Contact us and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
