Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Aura Scale (operated by AL-SHAMKHI TECH LTD), we respect your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order Information: Managed via Paddle when purchasing a license (email, name, billing details).
  • Activation Data: One-time activation requires verifying your license key with our verification endpoint. No personal images or processed files are ever uploaded or transmitted.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics on our website to understand visitor interactions.

2. How Your Images Are Processed

Aura Scale is a 100% local desktop processing tool. Your images never leave your computer and are never stored on any remote server.

3. Third-Party Processors

Payments are securely processed through Paddle.com. Please review Paddle's Privacy Policy for details on payment data handling.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at support@aurascale.uk.
